      Limits of the estimate

      • Every number here is a model estimate, not measured. The Garred simple diffusion model fitted to one PET has not been validated as a prescribing model.
      • Predicted UF comes from an open three-pore model with typical-patient membrane parameters for the transport type. It is labelled experimental: against the published Akonur 2016 (PDI 36:79-84) predictions it is off by up to about 250 mL per period (see About UF). It never changes Kt/V or the step 2 order; it does order the step 4 UF suggestions.
      • Icodextrin UF is the published typical-patient value (Akonur 2016, PDI 36:79-84: 2 L fill, 9-h CAPD / 15-h APD dwell, 350 mL residual volume), not simulated and not adjusted to the regimen shown.
      • The Kt/V estimate ignores residual volume, fill-volume and posture effects on transport, and CCPD fill/drain time.
      • Without PET numbers, Kt/V is a typical-patient estimate from published parameters (Akonur et al. 2016), not measured, and not this patient’s membrane.
      • Urine volume alone is not converted to clearance; residual function counts only with urine and serum urea (and creatinine for CrCl).
      • ISPD 2020: “There is no specific clearance target that guarantees sufficient dialysis for an individual.” Confirm any change with a measured 24-h dialysate and urine collection.
