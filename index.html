Regimen options

Standard CAPD and CCPD prescriptions (Handbook of Dialysis; ADEMEX). Extraneal rows use the same diffusion model: icodextrin’s UF advantage is not modeled, so they match the dextrose rows unless you enter expected long-dwell UF. Short dwells count net UF as 0.

Clearance vs targets

ISPD 2020: “There is no specific clearance target that guarantees sufficient dialysis for an individual.” 1.7 is the KDOQI 2006 minimum, shown for reference, not as pass/fail.

Limits of the estimate

  • The regimen estimate is the Garred simple diffusion model fitted to one PET. It has not been validated as a prescribing model.
  • Ultrafiltration is not predicted. UF counts only when you enter it.
  • It ignores residual volume, fill-volume and posture effects on transport, and CCPD fill/drain losses unless entered.
  • Transport type alone uses typical values for that type (Twardowski bands), not the patient’s own membrane. Kt/V needs a measured 4-h D/P urea.
  • Icodextrin (Extraneal) kinetics are not modeled.
  • Confirm any change with a measured 24-h dialysate and urine collection.
