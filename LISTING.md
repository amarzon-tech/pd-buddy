## Description
PD Buddy is an educational peritoneal dialysis (PD) prescribing tool for nephrologists, published by Wellness Tech LLC.

Work through four steps:
• Patient: age, sex, height and weight, with body water and body surface area.
• Start prescription: classify peritoneal transport from a PET (or pick a transport type), then compare CAPD and CCPD options ranked against your weekly Kt/V reference value, with model estimates of Kt/V, creatinine clearance and experimental ultrafiltration.
• Check adequacy: enter the current prescription and a 24-hour dialysate and urine collection to see measured weekly Kt/V and creatinine clearance, and refit the model.
• Troubleshoot and summarize: a prescription summary you can copy or print.

Every number the app calculates is a model estimate, not a measurement, and each formula and source is listed in the app. Very low PET values outside the model's range are blocked rather than estimated.

Scan lab report: photograph a printed lab report and the app reads the values on your device. The photo is never saved or sent, and every value must be confirmed before it is used.

Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no data collection. Inputs are never stored or sent.

EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY. NOT MEDICAL ADVICE. PD Buddy is not a medical device and is not cleared or approved by the FDA. Its estimates do not replace measured adequacy, clinical judgment or your unit protocol.
