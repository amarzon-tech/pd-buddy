## Description
Peritoneal Dialysis Rx (PD Buddy) is an educational peritoneal dialysis prescribing tool for nephrologists, published by Wellness Tech LLC.

Work through four steps:
• Patient: age, sex, height and weight, with body water and body surface area.
• Start prescription: classify peritoneal transport from a PET (or pick a transport type), then compare CAPD and CCPD options ranked against your weekly Kt/V reference value, with model estimates of Kt/V, creatinine clearance and experimental ultrafiltration.
• Check adequacy: enter the current prescription and a 24-hour dialysate and urine collection to see measured weekly Kt/V and creatinine clearance, and refit the model.
• Troubleshoot and summarize: a prescription summary you can copy or print.

Every number the app calculates is a model estimate, not a measurement, and each formula and source is listed in the app. Very low PET values outside the model's range are blocked rather than estimated.

Scan lab report: photograph a printed lab report and the app reads the values on your device. The photo is never saved or sent, and every value must be confirmed before it is used.

Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no data collection. Inputs are never stored or sent.

EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY. NOT MEDICAL ADVICE. Peritoneal Dialysis Rx is not a medical device and is not cleared or approved by the FDA. It is an educational clinician tool. Its estimates do not replace measured adequacy, clinical judgment or your unit protocol.

## Review notes (App Review Information)

Peritoneal Dialysis Rx (home-screen name PD Buddy) is a fully offline educational calculator for nephrologists. It is a Capacitor iOS app that bundles its HTML, CSS and JavaScript (including the Tesseract.js OCR engine and English language data) in the app binary (ios/App/App/public/). It does not load a remote website, does not require a network connection, and has no login, ads, or analytics. The camera is used only for the on-device lab-report scan. No account is needed to review the app.

The content was signed off by Dr Amar Damodar (nephrologist) on 18 November 2026 at 9:09 PM ET. Every formula and citation is listed in the in-app About sheets and in CONTENT_REVIEW.md, which is included in the app bundle.

Demo path for reviewers (no account needed):
1. On step 1 (Patient), enter a sample adult: male, age 55, height 5 ft 10 in, weight 80 kg. Select transport type High-average. Enter residual urine 500 mL/day. (The in-app “Try an example patient” button fills the same values.)
2. On step 2 (Start Rx), CAPD and CCPD options appear, ranked, with model estimates of weekly Kt/V, creatinine clearance and experimental ultrafiltration.
3. Tap a top CAPD or CCPD option to open the prescription summary, which you can Copy or Print.
4. On step 3 (Adequacy), you may choose the current prescription and enter a 24-hour collection, or tap Scan lab report. If you use the camera, the photo is read on the device, is never saved or sent, and every value must be confirmed before it is used.
5. On step 4 (Troubleshoot), pick a problem (clearance, ultrafiltration, or residual kidney function) to see suggestions.
